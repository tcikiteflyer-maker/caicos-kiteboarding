Your instructor

Hunter. Long Bay since 2013.

Hunter has been flying kites for twenty-nine years. He has taught kiteboarding on Long Bay since 2013 — the longest-serving instructor in the Turks and Caicos. Before this island he owned a kite school in British Columbia, Canada. The bar in your hands is held by someone who has already spent a lifetime in the wind. He holds IKO credentials.

Hundreds of hours of paragliding and paramotoring sit behind that coaching. He has competed, and won, in stunt kites, sport kites, and cross-country paragliding. He has judged professional kiteboarding competitions. The lesson is kiteboarding. The background is flight.
