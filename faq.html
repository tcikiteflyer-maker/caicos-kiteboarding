What is kiteboarding?

A kite on four lines, a harness at your waist, and a board under your feet. The wind does the pulling. You start on a twin-tip in the flat, shallow water at Long Bay. A directional board comes later, once you can already ride.

Is kiteboarding safe?

It is a real sport. With a modern kite, a safety system, and an instructor, it is one of the more manageable ways to learn a wind sport. Lessons start with how the kite flies and how to stop it, before anyone tries to ride away.

How long until I am riding?

Most people need about 6 to 12 hours before they are comfortable on the board. A few stand up on the first lesson. Plan the first beginner lesson at 3 hours.

Do I need to be strong?

No. The harness takes the pull. You steer the kite with small movements, not upper-body strength. It works your core. People of very different ages and fitness learn it.

Who should not kiteboard?

Skip it if you are pregnant, or if a neck, back, or other medical problem means you should stay out of a physical water sport. If you are unsure, say so when you request a time.

Do I need to be a good swimmer?

Comfort in the water helps. Most of the lesson is in waist-deep water on Long Bay, so you can stand up.

What should I bring?

The kite, board, harness, and helmet are included. Bring yourself, sunscreen, and something you can swim in. A rash guard is worth packing. Leave jewelry on shore.

I do not have a car.

Taxis run on Providenciales, and a hotel or villa can book one. Lessons meet on Long Bay Beach. Hunter confirms the exact spot when he confirms the time.

Where are the lessons?

Long Bay Beach, on the south side of Providenciales. Shallow turquoise water, sandy bottom. The exact stretch depends on the wind and the tide that day.

How long is a lesson?

Two hours minimum. A first beginner lesson is best at 3 hours. After that, book as many hours as you want.

How windy does it need to be?

About 12 knots or more, or there is no lesson. We do not do a beach-only theory session on a day you will not fly a full-size kite. The weather page has the season and the forecasts. Hunter calls about an hour before if the day is off. Full refund if a lesson is cancelled for weather.

Who teaches?

Hunter. He has been flying kites for 29 years and has taught kiteboarding on Long Bay since 2013. He holds IKO credentials.

How much are lessons?

$150 an hour for one student. Two students with one instructor is $225 an hour. Equipment is included. Sending the form is a request. Hunter writes back if that time is open.

Do we get in the water right away?

Not on a first lesson. You learn the wind and the kite on the beach, then the safety systems, then the water.

Can kids learn? How old is too old?

Weight matters more than age. A rider needs to be heavy enough for a small kite to pull them, and able to follow instructions in the water. Children can learn. So can people starting later in life. Put the age and weight in the request and Hunter will say if it is a fit.
