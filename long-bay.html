The spot

Shallow enough to stand a mile offshore.

Long Bay is known for water you can see through. It stays shallow enough to stand in, even a mile from the beach. There is room to learn without anyone on top of you, in about as comfortable a setting as this sport gets.

The trades blow from early November until early September. The stronger, more consistent wind is June through mid-August. A winter month averages about 21 good kite days. The wind, by month.

Most riders are on a 10 to 14 metre kite. A downwinder can run from Long Bay to Omar's Beach Hut, about 7 miles, in Five Cays. Beginners stay in the shallows.
