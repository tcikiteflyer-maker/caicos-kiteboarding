The spot

Long Bay Beach is the reason people learn here.

The south shore of Providenciales faces the east-southeast trades. The water stays shallow for hundreds of metres. The bottom is sand. On a proper wind day the bay fills with colour, but there is still room to work.

Kiteable from early November until early September. June, July, and August have the best wind. Air and water stay warm year-round. Most riders use 9–12 m kites. The wind, by month.

Downwinders can continue toward Turtle Tail and Five Cays when conditions and experience line up. Beginners stay in the shallows.
